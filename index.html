Six Ways to Experience the Village

01

Christmas Market

Wander through charming festive stalls offering holiday gifts, handcrafted products, local specialties, seasonal treats and Christmas-inspired shopping. Discover unique gifts while enjoying mountain views and the atmosphere of the village.

02

Festive Food Zone

Enjoy BBQ specialties, pilau, seasonal desserts, festive drinks and family-friendly dining options. Gather with friends and family in decorated spaces created for the warmth and joy of the season.

03

Hot Wine Experience

Gather around large warming pots gently simmering with wine, festive spices and seasonal aromas. Relax beneath Christmas lights with cozy blankets and enjoy the mountain evening atmosphere.

04

Christmas Cinema

Enjoy family-friendly Christmas films inside our festive cinema in the large event tent. Multiple daily showings create a warm holiday experience for families, couples and friends.

05

Live Entertainment

Experience a rotating festive program featuring live music, Christmas choirs, cultural performances, seasonal entertainment, family-friendly stage shows and interactive holiday activities.

06

Family Attractions

Christmas crafts, interactive experiences, supervised play areas, seasonal photo opportunities, children's holiday activities and the Bunny Kingdom bring wonder to guests of all ages.

Stay overnight and experience the fullest version of Christmas at Grotta. Enjoy the festive atmosphere into the evening, wake up in the volcanic highlands and make the celebration part of a memorable Musanze escape.
